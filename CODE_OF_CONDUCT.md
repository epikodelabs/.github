# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

EpikodeLabs is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for everyone participating in our projects and community spaces.

We value technical disagreement, critical feedback, experimentation, and strong opinions. They should be expressed in ways that help people understand the problem and move the work forward.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- criticize ideas, code, and technical decisions rather than people;
- assume good intent while remaining clear about problems;
- give others room to ask questions and make mistakes;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and boundaries;
- help keep technical discussions focused and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or discriminatory language;
- threats or intimidation;
- personal insults or sustained hostile behavior;
- sexualized language or attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately disrupting project discussions;
- repeated bad-faith engagement after maintainers have asked for it to stop.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in EpikodeLabs repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

It also applies when someone is representing EpikodeLabs in public project-related interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or keep discussions productive.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

For conduct concerns that should not be discussed publicly, contact the maintainers through a private channel listed by the relevant project or organization profile.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by widely used open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant, while intentionally remaining concise for the scale and needs of EpikodeLabs projects.
